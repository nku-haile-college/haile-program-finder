# Scoring Key

Each answer adds points to one or more programs. The program with the highest total is the best fit. Any program within 2 points of the top score is shown as a close second.

## Rules

- Every question is single-choice and required.
- **MBLI experience rule:** if Q5 is "Less than 1 year" or "1–3 years," MBLI is capped at 1 point below the higher of MBA or MAcc, and the results show a note about a future fall cohort. (MBLI requires 3+ years of professional leadership experience.)
- Negative totals floor at 0.
- **MAcc track suggestion** (from Q4): current NKU accounting undergrad → Expedited Track; accounting degree → Professional or Data Analytics/Visualization Track; any other degree → Accounting Practice & Reporting Track (APRT).
- Rebuilding in Qualtrics: use Scoring with three categories (MBA, MAcc, MBLI) and branch the end-of-survey message on the highest category. Google Forms has no multi-category scoring, so use a Sheets formula on the responses tab.
